Onam

Onam is a joyful harvest festival celebrated mainly in Kerala. It is known for beautiful flower carpets called Pookalam, traditional feasts called Onam Sadya, boat races, dances, and cultural celebrations. People wear traditional clothes and decorate their homes with flowers and lamps. Families come together to enjoy delicious food and celebrate with happiness. Onam is associated with the legendary King Mahabali and symbolizes prosperity, unity, and togetherness. It is a festival that fills Kerala with joy, culture, and colorful traditions.
