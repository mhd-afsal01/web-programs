Holi

Holi is a colorful and joyful festival celebrated across India. It is known as the Festival of Colors and is celebrated with bright colors, music, sweets, and fun gatherings. People play with colored powders and water, meet friends and family, and enjoy delicious traditional foods. Homes and streets become lively with colorful celebrations and laughter. Holi symbolizes the victory of good over evil and the arrival of spring. It brings people together and spreads happiness, friendship, love, and positivity..
