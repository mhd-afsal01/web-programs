Diwali

Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is a joyful Indian festival celebrated with lamps, colorful decorations, sweets, fireworks, and family gatherings. It symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. People decorate their homes with diyas, rangoli, and beautiful lights. Families wear new clothes, exchange gifts, and enjoy delicious traditional sweets and meals together. Diwali is also a time for prayers, happiness, and spreading love and kindness. The festival brings people together and fills homes with joy, hope, and positivity.
