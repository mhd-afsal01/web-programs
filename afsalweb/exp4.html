Famous Festival Of India

Diwali

Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is a joyful Indian festival celebrated with lamps, colorful decorations, sweets, fireworks, and family gatherings. It symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

Onam

Onam is a vibrant harvest festival celebrated mainly in Kerala. It is known for flower carpets, traditional feasts, cultural programs, boat races, and joyful family gatherings. Onam celebrates prosperity, unity, and the legendary return of King Mahabali.

Holi

Holi is the colorful festival of India, celebrated with vibrant colors, music, sweets, and joyful gatherings. It symbolizes the arrival of spring, friendship, and the triumph of good over evil.
